Privacy Notice

Song Request Panel · last updated 11 September 2026

This Twitch extension lets viewers request songs on twitch.tv/cheezeburgerstick. This notice explains exactly what it collects, why, and how long it is kept.

The extension is run by the broadcaster personally. There is no company behind it, no advertising, and no analytics. Nothing collected is sold or shared with anyone.

What is collected

If you choose to share your identity with the extension, it receives your Twitch user ID and display name. Sharing is optional and Twitch asks you first — but a song cannot be requested without it, for the reasons below. You can withdraw it at any time in your Twitch settings.

The extension also receives:

  • the song name or link you type into the panel
  • confirmation from Twitch when you spend Bits, including a transaction ID

Why identity is needed

  • To credit you on stream. Your display name is shown beside your song in the on-stream overlay and in chat.
  • To put things right if a paid request fails. Bits cannot be refunded once spent, so if a request you paid for could not be queued, a free replacement request is recorded against your Twitch ID.

What is stored, and for how long

  • Your display name and Twitch ID, alongside any song of yours that is queued or has recently played. This is removed as the queue moves on and is cleared when the broadcaster clears the queue.
  • Bits transaction IDs, so the same payment cannot be used twice. These are automatically deleted after 30 days. No payment details are ever seen by this extension — Twitch handles the payment entirely.
  • Free requests you are owed, if a paid request ever failed, until you use them.
  • Twitch IDs of anyone blocked from requesting, kept until unblocked.

All of this is stored in files on the broadcaster's own computer. There is no external database, and no cookies or tracking of any kind are used.

Who else sees it

  • YouTube. The song you type is sent to the YouTube Data API to find the video. Your identity is not sent with it. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Spotify, only if you paste a Spotify link, to read that track's title and artist. Your identity is not sent. See Spotify's Privacy Policy.
  • Twitch, which handles your identity and any Bits payment under its own privacy notice.

Nobody else. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Your choices

  • Decline to share your identity — you simply cannot request songs.
  • Revoke it later in your Twitch settings, under connected extensions.
  • Ask for your data to be deleted by messaging the broadcaster on Twitch. Requests already played age out on their own.

Contact

Message cheezeburgerstick on Twitch with any question about this notice or your data.
